      Methodology. Provincial HDI follows the revised BPS series published in November 2025 (Jakarta 84.15, Highland Papua 54.43); an older series circulating in some tables (Jakarta 83.08) is superseded. Poverty is the September 2024 rate from the BPS poverty BRS (national 8.57%). GRP per capita is nominal, 2024, in thousand rupiah. Note that the population-weighted mean of provincial GRP per capita (Rp83.10 million) sits above the national figure (Rp78.62 million) — provincial GRP sums exceed national GDP by design in BPS methodology, so the two are not directly comparable.

      Figures are from the World Bank World Development Indicators, BPS — Statistics Indonesia, UNDP and the CIA World Factbook. Each figure shows its own reference year; years differ by indicator.
