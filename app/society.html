Life expectancy fell, then came all the way back

Life expectancy at birth, years, 2015–2024. The pandemic cut nearly three years — 70.35 in 2019 to 67.45 in 2021 — then the series recovered to 71.29, its highest value yet.

Reading society

The COVID scar is visible in one chart. Life expectancy is the only headline indicator with a hole in it: three years lost in 2020–2021, then a full recovery by 2024. Few countries show the dip and the rebound this cleanly in a single series.

Schooling is the quiet success. Secondary enrolment rose from 87.8% to 98.8% while primary enrolment held at full coverage. Adult literacy, at 96.0% in the latest available year (2020), is the base that makes the rest possible.

Health spending is modest. At 2.7% of GDP, Indonesia spends less on health than most middle-income peers. Infant mortality has improved to 17.7 per 1,000 live births — but the level still leaves room to close the gap with the region’s best.
