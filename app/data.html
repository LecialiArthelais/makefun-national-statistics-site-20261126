Methodology. All World Bank figures are World Development Indicators (WDI vintage 11 Nov 2026), pulled directly from the World Bank API. BPS figures are from Berita Resmi Statistik releases. Years differ by indicator — each row shows its own reference year, so a 2020 literacy figure sits honestly beside a 2024 GDP figure. Derived figures (for example age-group populations, computed as population × share, and the CPI index on the Economy page, computed by compounding the published annual inflation rates) are labelled as derived. The population pyramid’s 45–49 band is derived as the residual of the published sex totals. Where a source disagreed with itself, the direct API value was used and the conflict noted on the relevant page: provincial HDI values follow the revised BPS series (Jakarta 84.15), and provincial GRP per capita is shown as published — the population-weighted mean of the provinces (Rp83.10 million) is above the national figure (Rp78.62 million) because provincial GRP sums exceed national GDP by design in BPS methodology. Provincial populations are BPS mid-year estimates for 2025; a province-level SUPAS 2025 table is not published (see the Regions page).
