Indonesia went online in a decade

Internet users, % of population, 2015–2024. The steepest verified trend in the country’s data: 22.1% to 72.8% — a 3.3× rise.

Source: World Bank WDI (IT.NET.USER.ZS), 2024. Values are the share of the population using the internet.

A $1.40 trillion economy

GDP, nominal, $ billion, 2015–2024. Steady growth, one sharp COVID dip, then recovery past the pre-pandemic level.

Real GDP growth, % per year. Ten years near 5% — except 2020, the only contraction in the series.
