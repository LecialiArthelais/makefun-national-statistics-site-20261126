Reading the population

The dividend is now. With 68.1% of people of working age and a dependency ratio of 46.8, Indonesia is in the middle of its demographic dividend — the window when the workforce is large relative to children and retirees. The 65+ share is only 7.3% today, so the window is still open.

Growth is slowing gently. Population growth was 0.81% in 2024 and fertility sits at 2.12 children per woman — just above replacement. The population still grows by more than two million people a year, but the pace is easing.

Geography is destiny. Java’s 55.65% share makes it the most populous island on Earth. West Java alone (50.76 million) has more people than most countries. That concentration shapes everything from infrastructure spending to election maps. Explore all 38 provinces →
