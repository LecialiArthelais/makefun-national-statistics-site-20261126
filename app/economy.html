Economy · 2024

A $1.40 trillion economy that grows at 5%

Indonesia is the world’s 16th-largest economy and the largest in Southeast Asia. Growth has held near 5% for a decade, exports run on coal, palm oil and steel, and the rupiah averaged 15,855 to the dollar in 2024.

Reading the economy

Steady, not spectacular. Five percent growth has been Indonesia’s normal for a decade: domestic consumption carries it, and the 2020 contraction was the exception that proves the rule — a single year, followed by a full recovery to $1.40 trillion.

Commodities still pay the bills. Mineral fuels are a fifth of goods exports, and palm oil alone is worth $22.68 billion a year. The newer story is downstream: iron and steel exports reached $25.80 billion as nickel processing scaled up.

Prices are calm. Inflation fell to 2.18% in 2024, its lowest in the ten-year series except the pandemic year. The rupiah, at 15,855 per dollar on average, is the number to watch when commodity prices turn.
